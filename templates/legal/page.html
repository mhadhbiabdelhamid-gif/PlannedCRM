Planned CRM is the internal business system of Planned Real Estate, a licensed real estate agency in Doha, Qatar ("we", "us"). These terms cover Planned CRM and its connection to social media accounts, including Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Who may use it

Planned CRM is used only by Planned Real Estate staff with an account we create. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the company can sign up.

What it does with social media

Our staff use Planned CRM to prepare and publish the company's own content (property listings, market updates, real estate news and tips) to the company's own social media accounts, at the time they choose. It publishes only to accounts that the company owns and has connected itself, and only content our staff have reviewed and approved.

Responsibility for content

Planned Real Estate is responsible for everything published from Planned CRM. Content must be accurate, must follow Qatar's real estate regulations and the rules of each platform (including TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, and Meta's terms), and must respect other people's rights.

Third-party platforms

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are run by their own companies under their own terms. Planned CRM uses their official interfaces and is not endorsed by them. A connected account can be disconnected at any time from Planned CRM or from the platform's own settings.

This policy explains what information Planned CRM, the internal system of Planned Real Estate (Doha, Qatar), handles when it is connected to the company's social media accounts, including Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Whose information

Planned CRM connects only to Planned Real Estate's own business accounts. It is used by our staff only. It does not collect information from members of the public through these platforms.

What we receive from the platforms

  • Account details: the connected account's ID, username and display name, so staff can see which account they are posting to.
  • Access tokens: the keys the platform issues so Planned CRM can publish on the company's behalf. They are stored on our server, never shown in full, and never shared.
  • Posting results: the status and link of each post we publish.

We do not read, store or analyse followers' data, messages, comments or viewing data.

How it is used

Only to publish the company's content to the company's own accounts and to show staff whether it was published. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Information is stored on our server with access limited to signed-in administrators. Connections to the platforms use encrypted HTTPS.

Removing the connection

An administrator can disconnect an account at any time in Planned CRM (Social settings), which deletes its access tokens and account details from Planned CRM. The connection can also be removed from the platform's own app settings. To ask for any information to be deleted, contact us below.
